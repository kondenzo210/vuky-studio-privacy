About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Vuky Flow – Weight Tracker, developed by Vuky Studio, handles information in the app.

Information stored by the app

Vuky Flow stores information you choose to enter, including profile details such as your display name and gender selection, weight measurements, weight goals and dates, measurement units, progress display preferences, and app language settings.

If Nutrition is enabled, the app can also store foods, portions, meals, calories, macronutrients, drinks, and saved meal templates. The FREE production version currently shows Nutrition as a preview rather than enabling the full module.

Weight and wellness information

Weight measurements and goals are entered voluntarily and are used to show your personal history and progress.

Vuky Flow is intended for personal weight tracking and general wellness. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or medical treatment.

Local storage and accounts

App data is stored locally on your device. No account is required. The app does not automatically upload app data to Vuky Studio servers.

Advertising, analytics, and telemetry

Vuky Flow does not use advertising SDKs or external analytics or telemetry services.

Limited technical diagnostics may be stored locally on the device without user content and are not sent to an external service.

Backup, export, and sharing

You can choose to create a JSON backup or CSV measurement export. Sharing happens only after you select a destination through the Android system.

The selected app or service then controls how it handles the file. Vuky Studio does not receive a file merely because you use the export or share feature.

Android system backup

Vuky Flow disables Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its app data. Use the app's manual JSON backup when you want a portable copy.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete relevant entries, reset measurements, delete a profile, or remove the app.

Deleting a profile removes its profile settings, weight data, and recorded meal entries. Some Nutrition records, including user-defined foods, saved templates, or drink-related records, can remain in the local database until they are separately deleted, deactivated, or the app data is removed.

Removing the app removes its local app data under normal Android behavior.

Security

Vuky Flow relies on Android app storage protections and limits file sharing to files you explicitly choose to export.

No method of storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, so keep exported backup files in a location you trust.

Children

Vuky Flow is a general wellness tool and is not specifically directed to children.
